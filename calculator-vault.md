## The short version

- **Your vault never leaves your phone.** Photos, videos, albums and file names are encrypted and stored only in the App's private storage on your device. We have no media server and never receive them.
- **No account, no sign-in.** We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
- **Your PIN and recovery key stay with you.** They are never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else. We cannot reset your PIN or recover a lost key.
- **Ads are provided by Google AdMob.** To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and similar data. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first.
- **We do not sell your personal information.**

## 1. Photos, videos and files you hide

The App processes only the photos, videos and files **you choose** through the system photo or file picker.

- Imported items are stored as **encrypted copies**, with encrypted thumbnails and an encrypted catalog (names, albums, favorites, import dates and deletion status), in the App's private storage.
- The App verifies the encrypted copy before it offers to remove the original. Removing an original always needs your choice and, where Android requires it, system confirmation.
- Viewing, playing or exporting an item may briefly create temporary decrypted files inside the App's private storage. The App deletes them when they are no longer needed, when the vault locks or on next start.
- **Exports** are decrypted copies sent to the place you choose. **Backups** are encrypted files you save and manage yourself; they need your recovery key to restore.
- Copies may still exist in system trash, Google Photos, cloud backups or other apps. The App cannot delete those for you.

## 2. PIN, encryption keys and recovery

Your secret calculator PIN unlocks encryption keys protected by your device's secure storage (Android Keystore). Optional fingerprint or face unlock is handled entirely by Android; the App never receives your biometric data.

Your PIN, recovery key and encryption keys are **never transmitted** anywhere. There is no online PIN reset. If you lose both your PIN and recovery key, your vault cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and funded by **banner and interstitial ads** from the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To serve, limit, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect:

- your device's **advertising ID**,
- your **IP address** and the **approximate location** derived from it,
- **device and app information** (model, OS version, language, app version),
- **ad interactions** (ads shown and tapped), and
- **diagnostic information** about the ads SDK.

Your hidden media, file names, album names, PIN and recovery key are **never** given to Google or used for ad targeting.

Google processes this data under its own policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- AdMob and user data: <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>

We receive only aggregated, non-identifying ad reports from Google.

### Consent

Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states), the App uses **Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to ask for your consent before ads are requested. If you do not consent, you may see non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your choice in **Settings → Help & Support → Ad Privacy Choices**.

On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in **Settings → Privacy → Ads**.

## 4. Ad settings service

When the App starts, returns to the foreground and occasionally while open, it downloads its ad settings (whether ads are on and which ad units to use) from a Google Apps Script web address over HTTPS. This request contains only normal network information such as your IP address. It **never** includes vault contents, file names, your PIN, recovery key or any vault identifier.

## 5. Permissions

The App uses:

- **Photos and videos** (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED`, or `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on Android 12 and older): to import the items you select and remove originals when you ask.
- **Biometric** (`USE_BIOMETRIC`): optional fingerprint or face unlock.
- **Internet / network state**: for ads, the consent form and the ad settings service.
- **Advertising ID** (`AD_ID`), added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

The App does **not** request contacts, SMS, call logs, camera, microphone or precise location. Refusing a permission only limits the related feature.

## 6. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

- **Vault features:** processed only on your device to provide the service you request.
- **Personalised advertising:** your consent, collected through Google's UMP form, which you can withdraw at any time.
- **Non-personalised ads, the ad settings service, fraud prevention and security:** our legitimate interest in keeping the App free and safe.

## 7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. The only third party that receives data from the App is **Google** (AdMob, UMP and Apps Script), as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it, but we hold none of your vault data.

**California and other US states:** sharing data with ad networks for cross-context behavioural advertising may count as "sharing" under some state laws. You can opt out by declining personalised ads in the consent form, by deleting your advertising ID, or by enabling your device's opt-out setting.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

- Vault items stay on your device until you delete them. **Recently Deleted** keeps items for 30 days by default (configurable) before they are purged the next time the vault is opened.
- **Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage permanently erases your vault.** Keep a backup and your recovery key if you want to restore later.
- Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies.
- No system can guarantee forensic or physical erasure of deleted data.

## 9. Security

The App encrypts your vault and protects its keys with Android's secure hardware where available. However, no app is immune to device compromise, malware, bugs or data loss. Keep your device updated, use a strong PIN and keep your recovery key and backups somewhere safe outside the device.

## 10. Children

The App is a general-purpose tool and is **not directed at children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
