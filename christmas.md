# Privacy Policy for Christmas Wallpaper

**Effective date:** October 27, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

Ganeshify ("we," "our," or "us") provides the **Christmas Wallpaper** mobile application (the "App"), identified on Android by the package name `com.ganeshify.christmaswallpaper`.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the App, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Information processed by the App

### Information stored locally

The App stores the following information locally on your device:

- wallpaper identifiers you add to Favorites;
- wallpaper identifiers recorded in My Downloads;
- onboarding completion status; and
- appearance, motion, and other App preferences.

This information is used to provide App features and is not uploaded by us to a Ganeshify user account or profile. The App does not require account registration.

You can remove this locally stored information by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. Wallpapers you save to your device may remain in your device's media library until you delete them separately.

### Motion sensor information

The interactive snow-globe feature may read accelerometer measurements while that feature is open. These measurements are used in real time to animate the snow globe. The App does not intentionally store or transmit these measurements.

### Network requests

The App connects to the internet to:

- retrieve the current wallpaper collection;
- download wallpaper images;
- retrieve remote advertising configuration; and
- request and display advertisements.

As with most internet services, servers and content-delivery providers involved in these requests may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, request time, device or browser information, and the requested resource. Wallpaper metadata is currently delivered through Google Apps Script, and images may be delivered by third-party image-hosting services.

## 2. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The App uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner, native, and interstitial advertisements when the relevant advertising placement is enabled.

According to Google's documentation, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information including:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
- user interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad or video views;
- diagnostic and performance information;
- Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and other device or account identifiers; and
- advertising-related information used to deliver, measure, analyze, and protect advertising services.

This information may be processed by Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit according to Google.

Depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google's configuration, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request updated consent information and show a consent form when required before requesting ads.

For more information, see:

- [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google advertising technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

You can manage or reset advertising identifiers and ad-personalization settings through your Android device settings and your Google account settings, where available.

## 3. Device access and permissions

The App directly declares the following Android permissions:

- **Internet access:** used to retrieve wallpapers, remote configuration, consent information, and advertisements.
- **Set wallpaper:** used only when you ask the App to set an image as your home screen, lock screen, or both.

Included libraries such as Google Mobile Ads may add permissions for network-state access, advertising identifiers, Android Privacy Sandbox advertising services, wake locks, and foreground services. These support advertising delivery, attribution, consent, fraud prevention, and reliable SDK operation. The presence of a manifest permission does not necessarily mean it is used during every App session.

When you choose to save a wallpaper, the App writes the selected image to your device's media library using Android's media-storage system. When you choose to share a wallpaper, the App passes the selected image to Android's share interface and the destination app you select. The privacy practices of that destination app are governed by its own policy.

The App does not request access to your contacts, microphone, precise location, phone calls, or SMS messages.

## 4. How information is used

Information is processed to:

- operate wallpaper browsing, preview, download, sharing, favorites, and wallpaper-setting features;
- remember your preferences on your device;
- retrieve and deliver current content;
- display and measure advertisements;
- obtain and respect advertising-consent choices;
- maintain security, diagnose failures, and prevent fraud or abuse; and
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 5. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information directly. Information may be processed by or shared with service providers necessary to operate the App, including:

- Google AdMob, Google Mobile Ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform;
- Google Apps Script or related Google infrastructure used to deliver remote configuration and wallpaper metadata;
- third-party image-hosting and content-delivery providers; and
- apps or services you deliberately select through Android's sharing interface.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. They may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

## 6. Data retention

Favorites, download identifiers, onboarding status, and App preferences remain on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. Images saved to the media library remain there until you delete them.

We do not operate a user-account database for the App. Retention of information processed by Google or other third-party providers is governed by those providers' policies and your applicable settings.

## 7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

You may also:

- clear the App's local data through Android settings;
- uninstall the App;
- delete saved wallpaper files from your device;
- manage advertising choices in your device and Google account settings; and
- use any consent or privacy options presented by Google's User Messaging Platform.

Because the App does not create user accounts, we may not be able to identify App data as belonging to a particular individual. Contact us if you have a privacy request or question.

## 8. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience personalization application and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly request names, email addresses, or other direct contact information from children through the App.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action. Advertising behavior may also depend on the audience and privacy settings configured in Google Play Console and AdMob.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the App, including HTTPS connections where supported by service providers. However, no electronic transmission, storage method, or service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. International processing

Third-party providers, including Google and image-hosting providers, may process information on servers located outside your country. Where required, such processing is subject to the safeguards provided by the relevant service provider and applicable law.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, third-party services, or legal requirements change. We will update the "Last updated" date above when changes are published. Material changes may also be communicated in the App or through its store listing when appropriate.
