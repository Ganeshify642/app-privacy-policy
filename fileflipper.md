This Privacy Policy explains how FileFlip handles information when you use its Android image and PDF tools, advertising features, and related services.

## 1. Your images and documents

FileFlip converts, compresses, resizes, and combines supported images into PDFs **on your device**. You do not need an account to use its tools.

- FileFlip does not upload your selected images, generated PDFs, file contents, filenames, or output paths to our servers, the advertising configuration endpoint, or advertising services.
- File access begins with files you select through Android's system picker.
- Generated outputs are separate files; FileFlip does not overwrite your originals.
- Image orientation is applied during processing and EXIF metadata, which can include GPS tags, is removed from generated images. Originals are unchanged.
- If you select a file from a cloud document provider or export a result to one, that provider handles its own storage and transfers under its policies.

## 2. Information stored on your device

FileFlip stores the following locally:

| Information | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Theme, output format, and quality preferences | Remember your settings |
| Up to 200 history records, including filenames, output references, operation, completion time, sizes, and dimensions where applicable | Help you find and manage completed results |
| Generated image and PDF files | Keep outputs available to open, export, share, or delete |
| Advertising frequency counters, impression timing, and rewarded quiet-session expiry | Apply advertising limits and earned benefits |
| Consent information managed by Google's consent SDK | Remember applicable advertising privacy choices |

Image and PDF binary data is stored as files, not inside preferences. FileFlip excludes its app data from Android cloud backup and device transfer through its Android backup configuration.

## 3. Advertising configuration requests

On each cold app launch, FileFlip makes an HTTPS request to a Google Apps Script endpoint operated for the publisher. This request retrieves FileFlip's Android advertising status and ad unit IDs.

The request does not include file contents, filenames, output paths, or app-supplied user identifiers. As with other internet connections, the hosting service can receive connection information such as your IP address and request timing and may process service logs under its policies.

Only enabled ad types with valid configuration may request ads. If configuration is disabled, missing, invalid, or unavailable, FileFlip does not send ad load requests for those types. File tools remain available without an advertising connection.

## 4. Google advertising and consent services

FileFlip integrates **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** and **Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)**. When advertising is enabled, advertising and consent features may communicate with Google. Google's SDKs may collect or share information such as:

- IP addresses, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
- device or advertising identifiers;
- app and advertising interactions;
- diagnostics, including SDK performance and crash information;
- consent status and applicable privacy choices.

This information can be used to provide advertising, measure advertising performance, maintain the services, and prevent fraud or abuse, as described in Google's policies. FileFlip does not include your private files or sensitive filenames in ad requests. The presence of advertising SDKs means we do **not** claim that the app collects no data.

Where applicable, Google's consent flow asks for advertising privacy choices. An **Advertising privacy choices** entry is available in Settings when required by the consent SDK. Advertising availability and personalization depend on configuration, applicable choices, and Google's services. Declining consent does not block the core file tools. You may also manage advertising-related settings through Android's privacy controls where available.

Watching a rewarded ad is optional. Its described benefit is granted only after the advertising SDK confirms the reward. Saving, opening, and sharing ordinary results do not require watching an ad.

Related policies and information:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google advertising privacy controls](https://myadcenter.google.com/)

## 5. Analytics and diagnostics

FileFlip does not currently have a configured Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or other app analytics reporting backend. Its application analytics abstraction does not transmit events. Google's advertising services may still process their own advertising and diagnostic information as described above. Android and Google Play may also process information under your device and Play settings.

## 6. Sharing, opening files, and feedback

When you choose **Open**, **Share**, or **Save as**, FileFlip gives the selected output to the app or document location you choose. File access is granted using Android's supported sharing mechanisms. The receiving service's privacy practices apply.

Feedback is shared only when you choose to share it. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send and use them to respond and address your request. Please do not send sensitive documents unless needed for a support issue and you choose to provide them.

## 7. Permissions and security

FileFlip uses Android's system file picker and scoped file access. It does not request broad access to your photo or media library or all files. Network access supports advertising configuration and advertising/consent services.

Remote configuration uses HTTPS, and generated files are stored in app-managed storage until exported. Device security and third-party services affect the protection of information; no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 8. Retention and deletion

- Clear activity metadata using **Settings → Clear activity history**. This preserves saved outputs.
- Delete managed output files using **Settings → Saved files & storage** or the output's delete action.
- History is limited to 200 records. Removing older history does not automatically delete its output files.
- Picker cache files are cleaned when leaving or resetting a workflow. Abandoned partial output writes are cleaned on a later startup.
- Uninstalling FileFlip removes its app-managed local data. Files you exported or shared may remain at the chosen destinations and must be managed there.
- Google and document/email providers retain information under their own policies and your applicable settings. We do not set or guarantee their retention periods.
- Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information you provided directly.

## 9. Your choices and requests

You can use the local file tools offline, choose which files the app accesses, delete local history and outputs, and use applicable advertising privacy controls. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights regarding personal information, such as access, correction, deletion, or objection. Contact us using the address below to make a request. We do not require an account and may be unable to identify information that is held only on your device or by a separate provider.

## 10. Children's privacy

FileFlip is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information through the file tools. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review the issue and take appropriate action. Advertising and audience settings must follow the app's declared audience and applicable requirements.

## 11. International processing

Your files are processed locally by FileFlip. Google, document providers, and email providers may process the information they receive in countries other than yours under their policies and applicable safeguards.
