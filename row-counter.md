# Privacy Policy for Row Counter: Knitting, Crochet

**Effective date:** November 14, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 14, 2026

Row Counter: Knitting, Crochet ("Row Counter," the "App") is provided by **Ganeshify** ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information is handled when you use the App on Android, why, and the choices you have.

By using the App you also agree to our [Terms and Conditions](https://ganeshify642.github.io/app-privacy-policy/row-counter-tc).

---

## The short version

- **No account.** You never sign up or log in, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
- **Your projects stay on your phone.** Project names, counts, notes, reminders and history are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
- **Backups are your own files.** A backup is created only when you tap "Export backup," and you choose where it goes.
- **Ads** in the free version are served by Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent first.
- **Remove ads** with a one-time purchase through Google Play. After that, the App does not start the advertising SDK.
- **No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or storage access.**

---

## 1. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following on your device so its features work:

- your projects (name, craft type, colour tag, target rows, notes);
- counters, counts, links between counters and undo history;
- row reminders and the time you spend counting;
- settings (theme, language, hands-free counting, vibration, sound, keep-screen-on);
- a record of whether you own the "Remove ads" upgrade, whether an ad-free period is active, your ad-consent status, and simple counters used to space out ads and review prompts.

This information **is not uploaded by the App** and is not accessible to us. It is deleted when you delete a project, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

## 2. Backups you create

When you tap **Export backup**, the App creates a file containing your projects and opens Android's share sheet. You decide where the file goes (for example Google Drive, email or your Files app); that service's own privacy policy then applies to the file. **Import backup** reads a file you pick with Android's file picker. The App needs no storage permission.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner, native, interstitial and optional rewarded ads served by **Google AdMob** (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). To show, limit and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app-set ID;
- your IP address, and approximate location derived from it;
- information about your device, operating system and the App;
- ad interactions (for example impressions, clicks and rewarded-video completion).

**Consent.** In regions where consent is required (EEA, UK and Switzerland), the App shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy settings**. In the United States, applicable state privacy choices are handled through the same consent tools where Google provides them.

**Your controls.** You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android **Settings → Privacy → Ads**. Ads are never shown during your first session, when you are offline, or after you buy "Remove ads." A rewarded video, which you choose to watch, gives 3 ad-free hours.

More information: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Ad settings downloaded by the App

When the App starts and when your device comes back online, it downloads a small settings file from a Google Apps Script web address operated by us. The file tells the App which ad formats are switched on and which ad unit IDs to use. **The request sends no personal data, no identifiers and none of your project data.** Like any web request, it reveals your IP address and basic technical information (such as the time of the request) to Google, which hosts the service. We do not use this to identify you.

## 5. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

If a build of the App includes Google Firebase, it uses **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to understand which features are used and to fix crashes:

- **Analytics** records anonymous usage events such as "project created," "backup exported" or "hands-free counting used," together with an app-instance identifier and basic device information. **We never send project names, notes or any text you type.** Analytics is configured so that it is not used for advertising (ad storage and ad personalisation are turned off).
- **Crashlytics** sends crash reports (stack trace, device model, Android version, App version) so we can fix bugs.

See [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 6. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional one-time **"Remove ads"** upgrade is sold and processed by **Google Play**. We never see your payment card or billing details; Google's terms and privacy policy apply to the payment. The App only learns whether you own the upgrade and stores that on your device.

## 7. Ratings and feedback

The App may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review screen; your rating goes directly to Google Play. If you tap **Send feedback**, your email app opens with our address, and we receive only what you choose to send. We use feedback emails only to reply to you and improve the App, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

## 8. Permissions

- **Internet and network state:** to load ads, download the ad settings, check purchases and (if included) send analytics and crash reports.
- **Advertising ID:** used by AdMob (section 3).
- **Billing:** for the optional one-time purchase.

The App does **not** request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, calendar or notifications.

## 9. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with anyone, except as described above: Google (AdMob, Google Play, Firebase, Google Apps Script) processes the data described in sections 3–6 to provide those services. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of users.

## 10. Data retention

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies. Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

## 11. Security

Communication with Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your projects are never sent anywhere by the App. No method of storage or transmission is 100 % secure, so keep backup files you export in a place you trust.

## 12. Children

Row Counter is a general-audience craft tool and is **not directed at children under 13** (or the higher minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

## 13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we do not hold your project data or any account, most of these requests are handled on your device:

- **Withdraw or change ad consent:** Settings → Privacy settings (where shown).
- **Reset or delete your Advertising ID:** Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
- **Delete everything:** Android Settings → Apps → Row Counter → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App.

For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

## 14. International transfers

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).
