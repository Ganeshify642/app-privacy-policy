# Privacy Policy for PromptPic

**Effective date:** October 23, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 23, 2026

PromptPic is provided by **Ganeshify** ("PromptPic," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the PromptPic mobile application (the "App").

PromptPic is a visual library of ready-made AI image prompts. The App does not generate images, does not require an end-user account, and does not upload or process your personal photos.

## 1. Information handled by the App

### Information stored locally on your device

The App stores the following information on your device so that its features work:

- Prompt identifiers for prompts you save as favorites.
- Prompt identifiers for prompts you recently viewed.
- Your theme preference and whether certain interface hints have been shown.
- Premium-prompt unlocks for the current app session. These session unlocks are discarded when the App is completely closed.

This information is not tied to an account and is not intentionally uploaded to our servers. You can clear recent history within the App. You can remove the remaining locally stored information by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

### Content and network requests

The App connects to the internet to:

- Download published categories, prompt information, and prompt text from Google Cloud Firestore.
- Download example images selected and uploaded by the PromptPic administrator from Amazon Web Services (AWS) S3.
- Retrieve the current advertising configuration from a Google Apps Script endpoint.

When these requests are made, the relevant service provider may automatically process technical request information such as your IP address, device or browser information, request time, and network diagnostics. We use these services to deliver App content, maintain security, and operate the App.

The user-facing App does not use Firebase Authentication, Firebase Analytics, or Firebase Crashlytics. Firebase Authentication is used separately to restrict access to the private administrator panel and is not used to create end-user profiles.

### Advertising information

PromptPic uses Google Mobile Ads to display native, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and Google settings, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share information including:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
- Advertising ID, App Set ID, and other device or account identifiers.
- App and advertisement interactions, including app launches, taps, ad impressions, and rewarded-video views.
- Diagnostic and performance information, such as crash-related data, launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.
- Information used for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

Where required, PromptPic uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before requesting advertisements. If Google requires a privacy-options entry point for your region, you can review or change your advertising choices under **Profile > Ad privacy choices** in the App.

Google may use information received from the App to provide, measure, personalize, and secure advertising services in accordance with your consent and Google settings. For more information, see:

- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google advertising controls](https://myadcenter.google.com/)

### Clipboard

When you select **Copy Prompt**, the App writes the selected prompt text to your device's system clipboard. Clipboard information is managed by your operating system. Other apps may be able to access clipboard contents subject to the privacy controls of your device.

## 2. Information we do not request

The current version of PromptPic does not:

- Require an end-user name, email address, phone number, or account.
- Request access to your camera, photo library, contacts, microphone, or precise location.
- Upload your personal photos or send them to an AI image-generation service.
- Process payments or sell subscriptions inside the App.

If a future version introduces any of these features, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those practices begin.

## 3. How information is used

Information described in this policy may be used to:

- Load and display published prompt-library content.
- Remember your local preferences, saved prompts, and recent activity.
- Display and measure advertisements, including rewarded advertisements used to unlock selected prompts for the current session.
- Request and respect advertising consent choices where required.
- Protect the App and service providers against fraud, abuse, and security threats.
- Diagnose technical problems and maintain reliable service.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 4. Service providers and sharing

We use the following service providers:

- **Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore** to deliver published categories, prompt metadata, and prompt text. See [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform** to provide advertisements, rewarded-ad unlocks, consent messages, advertising measurement, and fraud prevention. See [Google's partner data explanation](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Google Apps Script** to deliver the remote advertising configuration used by the App. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Amazon Web Services S3** to host and deliver administrator-provided example images. See the [AWS Privacy Notice](https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/).

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. They may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

We do not directly sell personal information. However, advertising providers may process or share advertising information as described above and under applicable privacy-law definitions. Your available controls depend on your region, consent choices, device settings, and the provider's policies.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, to investigate fraud or misuse, or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of the App, subject to applicable law.

## 5. Data retention

- Favorites, recent prompt identifiers, and preferences remain on your device until you clear them, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.
- Premium-prompt unlock status is held only in memory for the current app session.
- Published prompt content and administrator-provided images remain in Firestore and AWS S3 for as long as needed to operate the prompt library. This content is not associated with an end-user account.
- Google, Firebase, AWS, and other service providers retain automatically processed technical and advertising information according to their respective policies and legal obligations.

Because PromptPic does not create end-user accounts or store a user profile, we normally cannot identify server-side records as belonging to a particular person.

## 6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent for personal information.

You can:

- Change available advertising consent choices under **Profile > Ad privacy choices** when that option is required and available.
- Manage or reset your advertising identifier and ad-personalization choices through your Android or iOS settings and [Google My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).
- Clear recent prompt history from within the App.
- Clear all locally stored PromptPic data through your device settings or by uninstalling the App.
- Contact us using the address in the **Contact us** section below.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Some non-personalized or limited advertisements may still be displayed where permitted.

## 7. Children's privacy

PromptPic is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age required in a user's country. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## 8. Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. Network communication with the services used by the App is performed over encrypted HTTPS connections. No electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process information on servers located outside your state, province, or country. Those locations may have data-protection laws that differ from those where you live. Where required, providers use legal safeguards for international transfers as described in their policies and terms.
