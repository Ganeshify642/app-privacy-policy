**Last updated:** November 15, 2026

Money Calc: Paycheck & Mortgage Calculator ("Money Calc," the "App") is provided by **Ganeshify** ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains in plain language what information is handled when you use the App on Android or iOS, why it is handled, and what choices you have.

By using the App you also agree to our [Terms and Conditions](https://ganeshify642.github.io/app-privacy-policy/money-calc-tc).

## The short version

- **No account, no sign-in.** We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any login.
- **Your numbers stay on your phone.** Salaries, mortgage amounts, savings, saved results and settings are stored only in the App's private storage on your device. We have no server and never receive them.
- **Ads are provided by Google AdMob.** To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and similar data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first.
- **We do not sell your personal information**, and we do not collect financial, health, contact or precise location data.

## 1. Information you enter in the App

Everything you type into the calculators, including income, tax settings, pension or 401(k) contributions, home price, interest rates, savings goals and bill amounts, is used only to calculate results **on your device**.

- **Saved results** and your **settings** (currency and theme) are stored locally using the operating system's app storage (Android SharedPreferences or iOS UserDefaults).
- This data is **never transmitted** to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, recover it or delete it for you.
- When you tap **Copy** or **Copy full report**, the text goes to your device's clipboard. Where you paste it is up to you.
- **Deleting your data:** remove saved results in the **Saved** screen (swipe to delete), or uninstall the App or clear its storage to erase everything.

The App does **not** ask for, read or store your bank details, payment cards, government IDs, contacts, photos, files, SMS, call logs, camera, microphone or precise location.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is funded by ads from the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)**. It shows banner, native, app-open, rewarded and rewarded-interstitial ads. Rewarded ads are **always optional**: you choose whether to watch one, for example to unlock a full report or to get a short ad-free period.

To serve, limit, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process:

- your device's **advertising ID** (Android Advertising ID, or Apple IDFA if you allow tracking),
- your **IP address** and the **approximate location** derived from it,
- **device and app information** (model, operating system, language, app version),
- **ad interactions** (ads shown, taps, rewarded ads completed), and
- **diagnostic information** (crashes and performance of the ads SDK).

Google collects and uses this data as an independent controller under its own policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- AdMob and user data: <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>

Ganeshify does not receive your advertising ID or any other personal data from Google, apart from aggregated, non-identifying ad reports (for example, how many ads were shown).

### Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws)

Where the law requires it, the App uses **Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to ask for your consent before any ads are requested, and before personalised ads are shown. If you do not consent, you may still see **non-personalised or limited ads**. Where this applies to your region, you can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy & ad choices**.

### Your advertising choices

- **Android:** Settings → Privacy → Ads (reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalisation).
- **iOS:** the App asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) only where applicable. You can refuse, or change it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

## 3. Permissions

The App uses only:

- **Internet / network state**, to load ads and the consent form, and
- the **advertising ID permission** (`AD_ID`), which the Google Mobile Ads SDK adds.

The App does not request any sensitive permissions such as contacts, location, SMS, phone, storage, camera or microphone.

## 4. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

- **Calculations and local storage:** these happen only on your device to provide the service you request (contract / legitimate interests). No personal data reaches us.
- **Personalised advertising and the related device storage:** your **consent**, collected through Google's UMP form. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings.
- **Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and security:** legitimate interests in keeping the App free and safe.

## 5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. The only third party that receives data from the App is **Google (AdMob / UMP)**, as described in Section 2. We may disclose information if the law requires it, but we do not hold any of your calculator data.

**California and other US states:** sharing data with ad networks for cross-context behavioural advertising can count as "sharing" under some state laws. You can opt out by declining personalised ads in the consent form (where shown), by resetting or deleting your advertising ID, or by turning on your device's opt-out setting.

## 6. Data retention and security

- Data on your device stays there until you delete it, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.
- Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies.
- Connections made by the ads SDK use encryption in transit (HTTPS).

## 7. Children

Money Calc is intended for **adults (18+)** and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App and personal data was collected through ads, contact us and we will help you contact Google to have it removed.

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA or India's DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold **no** personal data about you, most requests are handled by deleting the App's data on your device or by contacting **Google** about advertising data. We will help with any request. Contact us using the details below.
