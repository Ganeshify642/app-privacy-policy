## 1. What Row Counter is

Row Counter is a counting tool for knitting, crochet and other yarn crafts. It lets you create projects, count rows, repeats and stitches (by tapping, with your phone's volume buttons, or with a Bluetooth page turner or keyboard), set row reminders, keep notes, and export or import backup files.

Row Counter is a **helper**, not a pattern. It does not check whether your pattern, counts or reminders are correct, and it does not replace reading your pattern carefully.
