Block Puzzle: Color Blast (the **“Game”**) is published by **Ganeshify** (**“we,” “us,”** or **“our”**). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install or use the Game.

By using the Game you also agree to our [Terms and Conditions](https://ganeshify642.github.io/app-privacy-policy/block-puzzle-tc).

## 1. Information You Provide

The Game does not require an account. We do not ask you to provide your name, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information through the Game.

If you email us for support or a privacy request, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message. We use it only to respond, maintain support records, protect our rights, and comply with law.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game stores the following information locally on your device:

- gameplay progress and saved-board state;
- scores and best scores;
- sound, music, haptic, and visual-theme preferences; and
- advertising frequency counters.

This local information is not uploaded to a Ganeshify account. You can delete it by clearing the Game's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the Game.

## 3. Advertising and Automatically Processed Information

When advertising is remotely enabled, the Game uses **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** to provide banner, native, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google and its service providers may automatically process information such as:

- IP address and general location derived from the IP address;
- device, operating-system, language, and app information;
- advertising and app-instance identifiers where legally permitted;
- diagnostics, crash, performance, security, and fraud-prevention information; and
- ad impressions, clicks, reward events, and other interactions.

Because the Game is intended for a general audience that includes children, we configure all ad requests as **child-directed**, **non-personalized**, and limited to a **general-audience content rating**. These settings are intended to limit interest-based advertising and the use of certain identifiers. Google remains an independent provider and processes information under its own terms and policies.

Learn more:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google advertising controls](https://myadcenter.google.com/)

## 4. Remote Advertising Configuration

The Game contacts a Google Apps Script endpoint to retrieve whether each advertising format is enabled and the applicable advertising unit identifier. Google, as the hosting provider, may process standard connection information such as your IP address, request date and time, device information, and request metadata. The configuration request does not require your name or Ganeshify account details.

## 5. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain or manage advertising privacy choices. The **Privacy settings** option in the Game's Settings screen lets you open available Google privacy choices. Some options may not appear when they are not required for your region or age treatment.

You can also:

- reset local gameplay and preference data through your device settings;
- reset or restrict your device's advertising identifier through system settings, where available;
- use Google's advertising controls; or
- uninstall the Game to stop future collection through the Game.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. Contact us for assistance. For information controlled directly by Google, submit your request to Google through its privacy tools.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Game is designed for a general audience and may be used by children. It does not contain account registration and we do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information to Ganeshify. Advertising is configured for child-directed treatment for every user.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us. We will investigate and delete the information when appropriate. Parents and guardians should supervise children's device and internet use.

## 7. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed or disclosed:

- to Google for advertising, consent management, remote configuration, security, diagnostics, and fraud prevention;
- to service providers acting on our instructions where needed to operate or support the Game;
- when required by law, legal process, or a valid government request; or
- when reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, Ganeshify, our service providers, or legal rights.

The Game does not include social posting, public profiles, or direct user-to-user sharing.

## 8. Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the Game. We retain support emails only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, keep appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and comply with law. Third-party providers determine their own retention periods under their policies and legal obligations.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the Game. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. International Processing

Google and other service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their policies describe the legal mechanisms and safeguards they use for international transfers.
